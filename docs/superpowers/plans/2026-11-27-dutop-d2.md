## Approved design translation

- Black/terminal background, rounded thin muted blue-gray borders, cyan titles,
  off-white filenames, soft green sizes, subdued cyan percentages and gray ranks.
- Two panels at widths >=90; stacked below. Header `dutop <path>` with
  `ALLOCATED SPACE` at right when space permits.
- Panel titles show `FOLDERS shown / count` and `FILES shown / count`.
- Columns: rank, name, size, % of complete directory total. Ranks start at 01 in
  each panel. A subtle dotted divider groups every five displayed entries.
- Preserve name endings and `[LINK]` when shortening. Fixed right-aligned numeric
  columns; no bars or terminal/font configuration changes.
- On widths below 42 cells omit percentage, below 32 omit rank. Keep size and name
  whenever space permits, then safely clip at extremely small widths.
- Footer contains total and full counts, with hidden/symlink/percentage hints.
  Wrap footer facts to avoid silently hiding counts on smaller terminals.
- Zero-byte total: percentage `—`; partial scan: `% KNOWN`, clearly partial total
  and explicit denominator hint. Never label a partial percentage as complete.

## Review focus

1. Full-total denominator when `--top` hides entries; large u64 values and zero.
2. Partial reports must not look complete.
3. Middle truncation must retain link markers/extensions without breaking Unicode.
4. Colored and plain output must have identical geometry at narrow widths.
5. More than 99 ranks, huge top values, and empty/asymmetric panels stay bounded.
